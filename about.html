Facilities

RSA Chambers provides the following facilities:

  • In the Jaipur office there are 7 conference rooms with a capacity of 4 to 12 people that are available in and out of published working hours. Chambers' Policies

RSA Chambers operates to the following policy undertakings:

RSA Chambers is committed to equality and diversity in all aspects of its work. We do not discriminate on the grounds of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, or sexual orientation. Our Equality and Diversity policy is reviewed annually and applies to all members of Chambers, pupils, employees and those seeking to join Chambers.
RSA Chambers is opposed to all forms of unlawful discrimination. We are committed to promoting racial equality and diversity within Chambers. We recognise that discrimination may be direct or indirect, conscious or unconscious, and we take all reasonable steps to ensure that our practices do not disadvantage individuals from any racial group.
All barristers at RSA Chambers are regulated by the Bar Council of India and are subject to the Code of Conduct of the Bar Council of India. Our barristers provide independent, specialist legal advice and advocacy. We are committed to providing high quality, cost-effective legal services to all clients, whether instructed by solicitors or directly under the Direct Access scheme.
RSA Chambers provides a wide range of general services to instructing solicitors and direct access clients. Our clerks team is available Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 6:00pm, to assist with all aspects of instructing counsel, including obtaining quotes, managing listings, and arranging conferences. Out-of-hours urgent assistance is available on [RSA — fill OOH].
We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent matters, our clerks team will endeavour to respond as quickly as possible. Written advice and settled documents will be delivered within the agreed timescale. If any delay is anticipated, the instructing solicitor or client will be informed as soon as possible.
RSA Chambers operates a professional clerking service with dedicated senior clerks for each of our three offices. Our clerks have extensive experience matching the right barrister to your case and ensuring the efficient running of your matter. All clerks are trained to the highest professional standards and are committed to providing an outstanding service.
RSA Chambers is committed to transparency in all matters relating to fees and billing. Fee quotes are provided before work commences wherever possible. Fees are agreed with the relevant barrister and Chambers' clerks before instructions are accepted. Where fees cannot be fixed in advance, an estimate will be given. Invoices are issued promptly following the completion of work. Full details of our pricing structure are available from the clerks.
RSA Chambers has a formal complaints procedure. If you are unhappy with any aspect of the service you have received, please contact our Chief Executive in the first instance. All complaints are investigated thoroughly and we aim to resolve them within 28 days. If you remain dissatisfied, you may refer your complaint to the Bar Council of India or the Bar Council of India Grievance Redressal Committee.
